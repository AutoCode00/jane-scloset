Questions

Ask Miss Button

Is it free?

Yes! There's nothing to buy — no tokens, no payments. Saving, entering and voting are tiny BOT Chain transactions, so you only pay the network gas.

Do I need a wallet?

Only to save, enter and vote. Dressing up and making photo cards work without one. When you're ready, connect MetaMask and we'll switch it to BOT Chain for you.

Can I vote for myself?

Nope — the contract won't let you. You get one vote per day, for someone else's look.

What's saved on-chain?

Just the outfit recipe — 32 tiny bytes saying which hair, clothes, colours and stickers you picked — plus a short look name. Your browser rebuilds the clay picture from that recipe.

When does the day end?

At midnight UTC. After that anyone can crown the winner — the site does it automatically when someone opens the results.
